# Review notes — B.Form reference-led Arkkaya revision

The layout follows the supplied revised 17 October 2026 brief: warm white/brown, concise text, large project imagery, founders near the top, equal Architecture/Interior Design emphasis, project arrows and filters, grouped project galleries, a three-step process, contact invitation and consistent mobile layouts.

## Original assets now reused

The supplied website became accessible during the delivery correction. Its original logo, joint founder portrait, project names, founder names/roles, project descriptions and project photographs have been reused. The existing projects are The Neoclassical Villa, Modern Luxe Residence and Blue Reverie Lounge. Rooms in Modern Luxe Residence remain together in one gallery. The demo no longer uses the earlier AI-generated project visuals or a substitute text logo.

The two founder biographies follow the current site's text. Contact email and Instagram links follow the current site. Their business ownership/receipt has not been independently confirmed. Phone, WhatsApp and a detailed studio address were not provided by the source and are omitted. No testimonial has been invented. Exact brand palette values were not separately supplied, so warm colours remain proposed for review.

## JPEG delivery

`previews/` contains every homepage section and all ten complete pages in desktop and mobile views. `review.html` displays and links to the images. All JPEGs are also packaged in `deliverables/Arkkaya-All-JPEG-Previews.zip` with a manifest.

## Interactive behavior

Project arrows, filters, per-project galleries, gallery keyboard controls, service-to-enquiry preselection, mobile navigation and dialog closing are functional. Three 9:16 Reel concepts use original project stills with simulated chapter playback. They are clearly marked as still-image previews, not published videos.

Contact and Careers forms validate fields and show demo confirmations. The resume selector validates PDF type and the 5 MB limit locally. Nothing is submitted, saved or uploaded. The Privacy page describes this behavior.

Real enquiry delivery and confirmation of receipt, secure resume upload/storage if Careers is retained, final service scope approval, authentic Reels, a genuine review if desired, verified business/contact information and an approved legal privacy notice remain production requirements. They are not presented as completed capabilities of this review demo.

The original live website has only been read to obtain its public assets. It has not been edited or redeployed. All source changes and any publication are confined to the separate demo location.

## This reference-led revision

The actual supplied B.Form About page was inspected, including its public page structure, CSS/font settings, photography presentation and process graphic. Its defining references are a broad photographic opener, restrained navigation, a centred vision statement, five material tiles, a simple process timeline and cream/brown surfaces. Arkkaya adapts those ideas with its own text, project photographs and founder information. No B.Form imagery, people, logo, commercial content or theme code is shipped in this demo.
